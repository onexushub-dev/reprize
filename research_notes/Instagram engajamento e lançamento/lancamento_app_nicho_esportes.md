# Instagram launch playbook for a Brazilian sports replay app (Reprize): pre-launch, launch, post-launch

Research date: 2026-11-08. About 18 tool calls. Many sources are vendor blogs or secondary news summaries, and each one is flagged where that matters. The writer should treat any number from a vendor as indicative only.

## 1. Pre-launch tactics (anticipation, waitlist, DM automation, build-in-public, TestFlight)

### Takeaway
Comment-to-DM automation ("comente REPLAY e te mando o acesso antecipado") is now the standard Instagram waitlist mechanic. Vendors claim it converts better than link-in-bio, but I found no rigorous indie app case study with download numbers. The well-supported pattern is a short keyword, a waitlist phase of a few weeks, then a launch keyword that sends the store link.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor guides recommend a pre-launch CTA like "Comment WAITLIST and I'll DM you first access". One guide suggests running a waitlist keyword that collects emails for about three weeks, then switching to a launch keyword that sends the link by DM — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-automation-product-launches-waitlists); [Inro 2026 playbook](https://www.inro.social/blog/how-to-use-instagram-dms-for-a-product-launch-2026-playbook)
- Short keywords are recommended because fewer typos mean a higher trigger rate — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-automation-product-launches-waitlists)
- Instagram limits how many DMs an account can send per hour. On a busy launch post the tool queues the rest, and some DMs may not arrive — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-automation-product-launches-waitlists)
- Vendor funnel targets: comment-to-DM 90%+, DM-to-click about 50%. These are unsourced vendor figures — [Stormy AI ManyChat app playbook 2026](https://stormy.ai/blog/manychat-app-marketing-playbook-2026)
- Indie app example (secondhand): the Coherence app reportedly kept 15,000+ downloads going on a $2,000/month influencer budget combined with DM automation. No ManyChat-specific breakdown — [Stormy AI](https://stormy.ai/blog/instagram-dm-automation-influencer-marketing-indie-apps)
- Non-app ManyChat cases: Candace Junée reported +118% leads compared with landing-page opt-ins, and Mindvalley reported +522% masterclass sign-ups. Both are vendor-published case studies — [IoT ONE / ManyChat case](https://leaders.iotone.com/case-study/leveraging-instagram-automation-for-enhanced-engagement-and-lead-generation-a-case-study-on-candace-jun-eacute-e/c2485); [Asia Growth Partners / Mindvalley](https://asiagrowthpartners.com/case-study/mindvalley-s-success-with-instagram-automation-a-522-increase-in-masterclass-sign-ups/c2494)
- Story-reply waitlist templates (reply to a story with a word and get a DM) are an alternative trigger — [Replient template](https://replient.ai/en/direct-message-automation-template/story-reply-waitlist)
- Apple pre-orders let a new app go on sale for pre-order 2 to 180 days before release. Apple recommends a CTA "Pre-order on the App Store" with a direct link. Apple does not tell pre-order customers about date changes, so the developer has to — [Apple, Offering apps for pre-order](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/pre-orders)
- Trial Reels (shown only to non-followers first) are open to public professional accounts with at least 1,000 followers. Mosseri says trial reels "almost always" get less reach than regular reels, so compare trials with other trials and judge them on sends, likes and watch time — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-trial-reels-hurt-your-reach); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-introduces-wider-access-trial-reels-9493132)

### Inferences
- For Reprize: use a ManyChat keyword such as "REPLAY" or "LANCE" in every pre-launch Reel. The DM collects the email or WhatsApp number plus the sport played, and offers TestFlight or Android beta access. Pre-order (iOS) is a second CTA once the build is approved.
- A countdown sticker in Stories (for the launch date) plus "Me lembre" reminders works alongside the DM list. These are native features, so nothing to cite beyond platform behaviour.
- Build-in-public / founder-led content suits Reprize well. Field tests at arenas ("testamos na Arena X hoje"), before/after of the clip-marking feature, and "o que o beta pediu e a gente fez" are all natural content. Trial Reels become available once the account passes 1,000 followers. Before that, post regular Reels.
- A TestFlight beta (up to 10,000 external testers is Apple's documented cap, not verified in this session) gives a pool of first UGC creators who can post real clips on launch day.

- No verified Indie Hackers / Product Hunt case study tying an Instagram waitlist to app downloads.
- No Brazil-specific data on ManyChat conversion rates.

## 2. Launch week (sequence, collabs, pinned posts, giveaways and Brazilian law, store badges)

### Takeaway
In Brazil, a real "sorteio" (random prize draw) on Instagram run by a company needs prior SPA/Ministério da Fazenda authorization, filed 40 to 120 days ahead and using Loteria Federal results. For launch week, a skill-based "concurso cultural" (for example "melhor lance da semana" judged on criteria, with no purchase or download required) is the realistic option, but the SPA reads that exemption narrowly. Use Apple's official localized badges only, and switch from "Pre-order" to "Download" on release.

### Cited Findings
- Free prize distribution for advertising by sorteio, vale-brinde, concurso or similar requires prior Ministério da Fazenda authorization (Lei 5.768/1971, art. 1º). The Secretaria de Prêmios e Apostas (SPA) authorizes and supervises through the SCPC system — [VISU regulamento tool, citing SPA](https://visu.network/br/ferramentas/regulamento-de-sorteio/)
- Only a legal entity (CNPJ) can be authorized. Individuals with only a CPF cannot run commercial promotions, including on social networks. Influencers take part as partner companies with their own CNPJ — [VISU / SPA](https://visu.network/br/ferramentas/regulamento-de-sorteio/)
- The promotion cannot be advertised before the Certificado de Autorização is issued, and the certificate number must appear on all material. Requests are generally filed 40 to 120 days ahead — [VISU / SPA](https://visu.network/br/ferramentas/regulamento-de-sorteio/)
- No cash prizes (including PIX), and no rifas. For social-network promotions, random selection by apps or platforms is prohibited, and draws must use Loteria Federal results — [VISU / SPA](https://visu.network/br/ferramentas/regulamento-de-sorteio/)
- Exemption: purely cultural, artistic, sports or recreational contests with no chance element, no payment, and no link to buying or using a product (art. 3º, II). The SPA reads this narrowly. Praising the brand in an entry, using registration data for advertising, or limiting entry to existing customers can remove the cultural character — [VISU / SPA](https://visu.network/br/ferramentas/regulamento-de-sorteio/)
- Instagram's own promotion rules make the organizer responsible for official rules and approvals. Posts must release Instagram from liability, say Instagram does not sponsor the promotion, and must not encourage tagging people who aren't in the content — [VISU summary of Instagram rules](https://visu.network/br/ferramentas/regulamento-de-sorteio/)
- Lei 15.182/2025 (30/07/2025) amended Lei 5.768 (added §5º to art. 2º), but it does not appear to change Instagram draw rules — search summary of [LegJur Lei 5.768](https://www.legjur.com/legislacao/htm/lei_00057681971). Not verified in the primary text.
- Apple badges: use the "Pre-order on the App Store" badge during pre-order and replace it with the "Download on the App Store" badge everywhere after release. Use only Apple-provided localized artwork. Do not translate "App Store" or make your own badge — [Apple Marketing Resources and Identity Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines)
- Collab posts: Mosseri said ranking currently favors the account that creates the collab post over the one that accepts, so the account with more followers should be the originator — [onlinemarketing.de](https://onlinemarketing.de/?p=367892) (German secondary report)
- Trial Reels reportedly cannot be collab posts (attributed to Metricool, unverified) — [Postfa.st](https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels)

### Inferences
- Suggested launch-week sequence (D = store release day):
  - D-7 to D-1: daily Story countdown sticker; one Reel per day with a real beta clip and the comment keyword; collab Reel with 1-2 beta athletes, originated by the larger account.
  - D0: "Chegou" Reel with official badges (both stores; Google Play badge rules not verified here), set as pinned post #1; ManyChat switches to the launch keyword sending store links; DM broadcast to the waitlist; collab posts with partner arenas.
  - D+1 to D+3: tutorial carousel ("como marcar seu lance em 3 passos"), first UGC reposts, a Stories Q&A with the founders.
  - D+4 to D+7: kick off the "Lance da Semana" contest; recap Reel.
- Pin three posts: the launch Reel, the "como funciona" carousel, and the best UGC clip / contest rules.
- Giveaway decision: skip a random sorteio at launch unless SPA authorization was filed 40-120 days before. A "Lance da Semana" judged by a jury on stated criteria (plasticity, difficulty), with no download required to enter (accepting clips filmed with any camera), stays closest to the cultural-contest exemption. Have it reviewed by a lawyer, since requiring the app or praise of the brand can turn it into a commercial promotion.
- Prizes from partners (arena hours, a class with a professor) are better than products like alcohol, which is banned anyway.

### Gaps
- Google Play badge guidelines were not fetched (known to exist at play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges; verify).
- The SPA fee (taxa de fiscalização) and penalty amounts were not found.
- Official SPA page not fetched directly; the rules above come via VISU's summary of the SPA. Verify at gov.br/fazenda.

## 3. UGC and community (watermark, reposts, "lance da semana", how comparable brands use Instagram)

### Takeaway
The Brazilian court-replay category already exists, with hardware-in-arena players (Gravae, VISU Replay). Their whole loop is "clip of the play, shared on social". Reprize's difference is the phone-on-tripod model with no arena hardware. Its Instagram should run the same share loop: a branded watermark or end-card on exported clips, reposts with credit, and a weekly best-play format.

### Cited Findings
- Gravae (startup from Bahia) installs cameras on courts. One button press captures the previous 30 seconds of a play for replay, download or link sharing. It reports 800+ clients, 300,000 registered users, and Neymar Jr. as a client. Coverage says it uses AI to automatically identify and deliver highlights for social sharing, with reported revenue of about R$9.5M — [Portal Tela, June 2026](https://www.portaltela.com/esporte/outros-esportes/2026/06/12/startup-baiana-usa-neymar-e-ia-para-filmar-quadras-e-faturar-r-95-mi/); [Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/76769-startup-baiana-focada-em-registros-esportivos-cresce-conquista-mercados-globais-e-craques-brasileiros) (2,000+ cameras installed nationally; figures differ between sources)
- VISU (Brazil) offers VISU Replay ("turns gameplay into clips to share") alongside VISU Link, Local and Ads, including "Replay Ads" — so arena replay clips double as an ad inventory. It also publishes SEO content like "como gravar sua partida de beach tennis" — [VISU](https://visu.network/br/ferramentas/regulamento-de-sorteio/); [VISU article](https://visu.network/br/artigos/como-gravar-sua-partida-de-beach-tennis/)
- Strava: its branded Instagram leans on UGC and creator storytelling, spotlighting real members (third-party summary) — [CreatorDB](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/strava/). Its 2014 "Year in Sport" built a personal shareable highlights film per athlete from API data — [Stink Studios](https://stinkstudios.com/work/your-2014-story)
- Veo: Player Profiles let athletes add their latest highlights straight from the Veo Editor, so highlights become identity assets players want to show — [Veo](https://veo.co/role/player)
- CONAR 2026 treats spontaneous user content as a separate category from advertising. Once a brand pays or gives gifts, disclosure applies (see section 4) — [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/conar-atualiza-guia-de-publicidade-com-influenciadores-e-inclui-regras-sobre-ia/)

### Inferences
- Product: export clips with a subtle "Gravado com Reprize" corner mark plus a 1-second end card with @reprize. Make the watermark removable on PRO, as many creator apps do. That gives free-user clips organic attribution, but test that it doesn't hurt sharing.
- Repost workflow: ask permission by DM ("podemos repostar com crédito?"), keep a screenshot of consent, and credit the athlete with an @ in the caption. Collab-invite the athlete so the post appears on both profiles.
- Formats: "Lance da Semana" (Friday, community-voted or jury-picked), "Erro da Semana / bloopers" (entertainment), "Antes x Depois" (raw full game compared with Reprize's 5 marked plays), "Ponto do professor" (coach breaks down a clip).
- Hashtags: one branded tag (#lancereprize or #reprize) plus sport tags (#beachtennis, #futevolei, #voleidepraia, #padel). Hashtag volumes were not verified in this session.
- Position against Gravae/VISU: "sem câmera instalada, qualquer quadra, seu celular". Arenas that already have Gravae are less likely partners, and arenas without it are the target.

### Gaps
- No direct analysis of the Instagram accounts of Gravae, VISU, Hudl, Mevo, Trace, Playtomic or To The Box (no live Instagram access; Playtomic searches returned nothing).
- Instagram hashtag post counts for #beachtennis / #futevolei were not retrieved.

## 4. Partnerships (micro-influencers and athletes, arenas, coaches, tournaments, CONAR and "publi" rules)

### Takeaway
CONAR published a new influencer guide in May 2026 that replaces the 2020 one. Paid or gifted content must be clearly identified, with the platform's native "parceria paga" label as the main tool and #publi / #publicidade as complements, visible without tapping "mais". Gifts (permuta) must have their origin disclosed. Reliable BRL price tables for micro creators do not exist publicly. The best available indications are about R$1,200 for a post plus stories combo at up to 20k followers, and Reels costing 2-3x a static post (both weak sources).

### Cited Findings
- CONAR released a new "Guia de Marketing e Publicidade por Influenciadores Digitais" in May 2026, replacing the 2020 guide. It took effect 13 May 2026; other outlets cite 1 June 2026 as the adaptation deadline (sources conflict) — [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/conar-atualiza-guia-de-publicidade-com-influenciadores-e-inclui-regras-sobre-ia/); [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/conar-atualiza-guia-para-influenciadores-digitais); [AMD Jus](https://amdjus.com.br/novas-regras-do-conar-influenciadores-devem-se-adaptar-ate-junho/)
- Disclosure: use clear terms ("publicidade", "publi", "publipost"), prominently, not hidden behind "mais". The 2026 edition prioritizes the platform's native tools (the "parceria paga" label), with #publi / #publicidade as complements — [SMA](https://smabr.com/?p=8460); [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/conar-atualiza-guia-de-publicidade-com-influenciadores-e-inclui-regras-sobre-ia/)
- The guide separates three categories: advertising, "mensagens ativadas" (gifts, invitations, experiences) and spontaneous content. Items received even without a commercial deal must have their origin made explicit — [KLA Law](https://www.klalaw.com.br/conar-atualiza-guia-de-publicidade-com-influenciadores/)
- Affiliate links, coupons and affiliate programs count as advertising. AI-generated content follows the same rules. Advertisers should check an influencer's compliance history before hiring — [Mundo do Marketing](https://mundodomarketing.com.br/marketing-de-influencia-entra-na-era-do-compliance-apos-novo-guia-do-conar); [KLA Law](https://www.klalaw.com.br/conar-atualiza-guia-de-publicidade-com-influenciadores/)
- The original 2020 guide (for reference) — [CONAR 2020 PDF via Poder360](https://static.poder360.com.br/2020/12/Guia-influenciadores.pdf) (older)
- Pricing (BRL): a Brazilian FAQ (Jan 2026) says creators with up to 20k followers charge about R$1,200 for a post plus stories combo, with no primary source — [Black Pilado FAQ](https://faq.blackpilado.com.br/2026/01/quanto-cobra-um-influenciador.html). Another report says Brazilian per-post pay starts around R$50 for 1k-5k followers, with no clear date or method — [Jornal Opção](https://www.jornalopcao.com.br/ultimas-noticias/homenageados-na-camara-saiba-quem-sao-os-influencers-que-mais-ganham-dinheiro-no-brasil-e-no-mundo-387353)
- Reels typically cost 2-3x a static post (international data, in EUR) — [Influee 2026 pricing](https://influee.co/pt/blog/instagram-influencer-pricing). Price drivers are niche, usage rights, exclusivity and scope — [CreatorsJet](https://www.creatorsjet.com/PT-BR/blog/taxas-de-influenciadores-por-plataforma-e-numero-de-seguidores-baseado-em-12000-campanhas)
- YouPix has no public 2025 per-tier pricing study; its toolbox lists "ROI & Influência 2024" (with Nielsen) and "Efeito da Influência no Consumo" 2025, with no rates — [YouPix toolbox](https://www.youpix.com.br/toolbox)
- Beach tennis industry structure: big arena circuits with sponsors (for example Lacoste with Arenas BTG) show arenas and circuits act as media hubs — [Máquina do Esporte](https://maquinadoesporte.com.br/beach-tennis/lacoste-anuncia-renovacao-de-patrocinio-as-arenas-btg/)
- Futevôlei: the CBFv hired the agency TFW to structure a national calendar with at least four National Championship stages, so tournament coverage partnerships are possible — [Máquina do Esporte](https://maquinadoesporte.com.br/futevolei/tfw-e-nova-agencia-de-marketing-esportivo-da-confederacao-brasileira-de-futevolei/)

### Inferences
- Tiering for Reprize: nano (1k-10k) amateur players and local professores at barter (PRO lifetime or a year of PRO + feature in Lance da Semana). Micro (10k-50k) regional athletes and coaches with a small fee, roughly R$300-1,500 per Reel bundle as a working estimate from the weak sources above. Validate with real quotes.
- Coaches (professores) are the best channel. They film students anyway, run several classes a day, and their content (correção de movimento) shows Reprize's value. Offer a "Professor Parceiro" program: free PRO, a referral code, and a monthly collab Reel.
- Arenas: free arena profile/QR at the court ("Marque seu lance — baixe o Reprize"), collab posts on tournament days, and a recap Reel of the arena's best plays each month.
- Tournament coverage: the team (or a partner athlete) films the amateur categories at regional circuit stages with tripods, and publishes same-day best-plays Reels as collab posts with the organizer.
- Compliance: every paid or bartered piece uses the "parceria paga" label plus #publi. Barter contracts should say so explicitly (CONAR treats gifts as "mensagem ativada").

## 5. Size and behavior of Brazilian beach tennis / futevôlei communities

### Takeaway
Beach tennis has about 1.1-1.2M practitioners (CBT, 2023-2024; a 2025 academic paper says about 2M, which is weak), and Brazil is described as the sport's world epicenter. Futevôlei has an estimated 500k practitioners in 1,000+ arenas (World Footvolley) and about 50M fans or interested people (Ibope Repucom, older). Both are arena-based and social-first, which fits a replay app.

### Cited Findings
- CBT: about 400k beach tennis practitioners in 2021 and 1.1M in 2023 (+175%) — [Máquina do Esporte](https://maquinadoesporte.com.br/beach-tennis/beach-tennis-atrai-elite-ao-pais-e-ve-aumento-de-175-no-numero-de-praticantes-em-um-ano/). A 2024 text cites CBT at 1.2M across all 26 states plus DF — [Diário do Comércio](https://diariodocomercio.com.br/negocios/entenda-por-que-o-beach-tennis-vem-ganhando-espaco-mg/)
- About 2M practitioners in 2025, from an academic paper (weak source) — [UNIVS paper](https://sis.univs.edu.br/uploads/12/2025/EF_139.pdf)
- Brazil is said to have passed Italy in practitioners and events and to have become the sport's world epicenter — [Folha Vitória](https://www.folhavitoria.com.br/esportes/beach-tennis-se-profissionaliza-e-brasil-se-torna-epicentro-mundial-da-modalidade/)
- "Beach tennis, um mercado sem dono" (market analysis) — [Brazil Journal](https://braziljournal.com/beach-tennis-um-mercado-sem-dono) (not fetched)
- Futevôlei: about 500k practitioners in 1,000+ arenas (World Footvolley estimate) — [Times Brasil](https://timesbrasil.com.br/esportes/crescimento-futevolei-brasil-negocios/). Ibope Repucom counts about 50M fans or interested people — [Máquina do Esporte](https://maquinadoesporte.com.br/outros/pesquisa-ibope-repucom-aponta-futevolei-com-50-milhoes-de-fas-no-brasil) (date unclear, likely older). - Geographic launch focus: beach tennis is strong in SP, RJ, ES, PR, SC and the interior of SP; futevôlei in RJ and the Northeast. Start with 2-3 cities and their arenas rather than a national push.
- Content behavior: these communities already post match clips. "Ponto incrível" and "erro engraçado" Reels are common. Reprize removes the "filmei 1h e não sei onde está o lance" pain.

## 6. Paid boost (when it makes sense, CPM/CPI in Brazil)

### Takeaway
Brazil is among the cheapest Meta markets. The median CPM is about US$3.46 and CPC about US$0.20 (vendor data, about Jul 2025 to Jul 2026), but I found no reliable Brazil-specific app-install CPI benchmark. Boost only Reels already proven organically (high sends and watch time), with small tests, and measure CPI in your own account.

### Cited Findings
- Brazil median Meta CPM about US$3.46 and CPC about US$0.20, compared with US$14.38 for Australia and US$13.05 for Canada. These are medians, so half of advertisers pay more — [Ads Uploader citing SuperAds](https://adsuploader.com/pt/blog/facebook-ads-cost); [SuperAds Brazil CPM](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/brazil)
- Brazilian CPC averaged about 80% below the global benchmark over the 12-month window — [SuperAds](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpc-cost-per-click/brazil/conversion)
- Generic CPI ranges: iOS about US$2-5+, Android about US$1-3 (not Brazil-specific, unclear method) — search summary of [Ads Uploader](https://adsuploader.com/pt/blog/facebook-ads-cost)
- Reels CPM about US$4-8 compared with Feed US$8-14 in DTC comparisons (global) — search summary, [Quality Unit](https://cdn.qualityunit.com/faq/good-cpm-digital-marketing)
- Brazil Meta ads playbook 2026 (not fetched) — [adlibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-ads-brazil-playbook-2026)

### Inferences
- Pre-launch: spend nothing, or R$20-30/day boosting the best Reel to "visitas ao perfil" or "mensagens" (feeding the DM waitlist) in target cities with sports interests.
- Launch: an app-install campaign (Advantage+ app campaigns) needs the Meta SDK / AppsFlyer-style attribution and enough volume. With a tiny budget, run a geo-targeted test of R$1,000-2,000 for 1-2 weeks, using Partnership Ads (whitelisted creator Reels) as the creative.
- Rough estimate: with a CPM around US$3-4 and a Brazilian iOS skew among beach tennis players (higher income), a CPI of roughly R$3-15 is plausible but unverified. Treat it only as a hypothesis to test.

### Gaps
- No Brazilian sports or utility app CPI benchmark for 2025-2026 was found.

## 7. Content mix, cadence and workflow for a 2-3 person team

### Takeaway
The data supports 3-5 feed posts per week (Buffer, 2M posts). For Reels, 2-3 per week had the best average engagement (Dash Social), and carousels are the most stable format (Socialinsider 2025: carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37%). For a small team, 3 Reels + 1-2 carousels per week + daily Stories, batched once a week, is realistic.

### Cited Findings
- 3-5 posts per week is the sweet spot for sustainable growth (Buffer analysis of 2M+ posts from 102k accounts, 2025) — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
- Engagement by format in 2025: carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37%; carousels were the most stable year over year — [Socialinsider 2025 benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/data-geeks/instagram_benchmarks_2025.pdf)
- Best average engagement by Reels frequency: 3.68% at 2-3 Reels per week (529k Reels, Jul 2024 to May 2025) — [Dash Social](https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/how-often-should-you-post-on-instagram)
- Suggested: 3-5 feed posts, 2-4 Reels per week, Stories daily or nearly daily (blog recommendation) — [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-posting-frequency-2025)
- Instagram's figure (secondhand): 40% of creators started posting Reels more often, and 80% of those saw more non-follower reach — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/do-trial-reels-work-research) (original Instagram post not found)

### Inferences
- Content mix (working proposal, not source-based): 40% community/UGC (Lance da Semana, athlete reposts, arena recaps); 25% product/education (how to set up the tripod, mark a play, export; PRO features); 20% entertainment (bloopers, trends with real clips, "tipos de jogador"); 15% founder/build-in-public (field tests, roadmap, beta feedback). In launch week, product rises to about 40%.
- Weekly cadence: Mon, a tutorial carousel; Tue, a Reel (founder/field test); Wed, a Reel (athlete or coach collab); Thu, a carousel or a UGC compilation; Fri, the "Lance da Semana" Reel; Stories daily (polls such as "qual lance ganha?", repost mentions, countdown before launch).
