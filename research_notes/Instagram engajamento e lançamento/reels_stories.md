# Instagram Reels and Stories: production best practices 2025-2026 (sports-tech app brand)

Research date: 2026-11-08. Note on sources: Instagram publishes few numbers. Most figures below come from vendor and agency blogs, often paraphrasing Mosseri, and are marked as such. I fetched two pages in full (Socialinsider Stories benchmarks, Hopper HQ Reel specs, plus the OpusClip hook article); the rest come from search-result summaries and should be spot-checked before anyone quotes them as hard data.

## Reels: what the ranking rewards, hooks and the first 1-3 seconds

### Takeaway
Mosseri's named Reels signals (Jan 2025) are watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Since Aug 2025 Instagram reports a "Skip Rate" (share of views that leave in the first 3 seconds), so the opening 1-3 seconds is now a metric you can measure directly. Hooks have to land visually and as text by second 1-2 and pay off by second 3.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri (Jan 2025) named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top Reels ranking signals. Likes weigh slightly more for followers ("connected"), sends slightly more for non-followers ("unconnected"). Reported via Social Media Today; no numeric weights published — [Hanami summary of Meta statements](https://hanami-social-website.pages.dev/en/blog/instagram-reels-algorithm-2026/); [Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm/); [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026)
- Mosseri reportedly said watch time is weighed both as relative (% watched) and absolute (seconds) — [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/how-does-the-algorithm-for-reels-work-in-2025/)
- Older (2024 Q&A) Mosseri quote, widely repeated: sends per reach correlates with reach more than any other signal — [Linqia](https://linqia.com/insights/how-the-reels-algorithm-really-works)
- Claims that "sends are worth 3-5x likes" or "original content gets 40-60% more distribution" come only from vendor blogs, not Instagram. Do not use them as fact — [CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026)
- Instagram added **Skip Rate** (percentage of views that scrolled away in the first 3 seconds) and a **Retention chart** to Reels Insights, replacing View Rate. Reported as Aug 2025. Instagram's explanation: a high skip rate means the opening didn't engage — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-retention-insights-reels/758464/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992); [marketing4ecommerce](https://marketing4ecommerce.net/en/retention-and-skip-rate-reels/)
- Instagram's own Reels guide (via Social Media Today): keep it "high-quality, original and engaging from the first second." Use text overlays to "create a hook that stops someone mid-scroll" — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-reels-creation-tips-in-new-guide/831551/)
- OpusClip (Nov 2025, author analysis, not a named study): Reels with a 3-second hold above 60% outperform those below 40% by 5-10x in reach. Target ≥60% hold, top performers 70-80%. Below 50% means rework the hook. Hook should grab within 1-2 s and deliver its promise by 3 s. 80/20 rule: proven formulas for most posts, test one new hook variant per week — [OpusClip hook formulas](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-hook-formulas)
- OpusClip hook templates: Contrarian ("Everyone says X, but Y" / "Stop X, do this instead"). Mistake ("I lost X because I Y" / "Don't make the mistake I made with X"). Numbered list ("3-7 [things] that [outcome]"). Time-based ("How I [result] in [short time]"). Question ("Are you [doing X wrong]?"). Curiosity gap ("The one thing nobody tells you about…", "I wish I knew this before…"). First frame: bold high-contrast text echoing the spoken hook + a close-up shot — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-hook-formulas)
- Emplifi study (summarized secondhand, not verified): speech in the first 3 seconds lifted 10-second retention by about 25%; early human presence (a face) added roughly 10% to 10-second views — summary in search results for [Pulse](https://www.pulse.bot/digital-marketing/news/human-speech-and-presence-help-drive-reels-engagement-26291baf-0b96-441f-aefa-8af987fa02e8) (fetch failed)

### Inferences
- Production rule for the brand: frame 1 must already be the action (ball on its way into the net, the replay's peak moment, a face reacting), never a logo or an intro. On-screen hook text appears at 0.0 s, not after a fade-in.
- Because sends drive reach to non-followers, ask while editing "would a player send this to a teammate?". A play-of-the-day, a funny miss, or a "tag the guy who always does this" moment beats a feature announcement.
- Use Skip Rate as the weekly KPI for hooks. Above ~50% skip means re-cut the opening.

### Gaps
- No official Instagram threshold for a "good" skip rate. The 60%/40% figures are OpusClip's.
- The Emplifi original study was not retrieved.

## Reels: optimal length, structure and loops

### Takeaway
The cap is 3 min (Jan 2025), with longer uploads for some accounts, but no reliable source shows longer Reels perform better. Watch-time signals favor short, high-completion cuts (roughly 7-30 s) for reach. Longer ones (30-90 s) are only for tutorials, where absolute watch time and saves justify it.

### Cited Findings
- Jan 2025: Mosseri raised the Reels limit from 90 s to 3 min ("we've heard the feedback that this is too restrictive") — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-swoops-in-with-3-minute-reels-and-rectangular-profile-grids-as-the-tiktok-ban-gets-real-201316339.html); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-to-reels-and-instragram-app-461527-2025-01-21)
- Hopper HQ (Sep 2026): 3 min when recorded in-app. Uploads up to 15 min for most accounts and 20 min for selected accounts. Some accounts are still capped at 90 s — [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/)
- AFP citing Instagram (May 2024, older): Reels longer than 90 s don't boost engagement — [The Star / AFP](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2024/05/15/instagrammers-avoid-making-overly-long-reels)
- Vendor claims: 7-15 s is the "optimal" short-form length; Reels under 15 s have a ~57% completion rate. Unverified vendor figures — summarized from [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-length) / [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length) / [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reels-length) search results
- Watch time counts both % watched and absolute seconds (see above), so a longer Reel can win if retention holds — [Stack Influence](https://stackinfluence.com/how-does-the-algorithm-for-reels-work-in-2025/)

### Inferences
- Length by goal (rule of thumb derived from the signals, not a measured table):
  - Reach / shares (highlight, funny moment, trend): 6-15 s, cut so the last frame flows back into the first (loop). Replays inflate % watched.
  - Saves / education ("3 ways to…", how-to in the app): 20-45 s with a numbered on-screen structure.
  - Conversion (app demo, "how Reprize clips your play"): 15-30 s. Problem (0-3 s) → product doing it (3-20 s) → result + single CTA (last 3-5 s), with the CTA also in the caption.
- Structure template: Hook (0-2 s) → Context/promise (2-5 s) → Payoff in beats every 2-4 s (cut, zoom, text change) → End on the best moment or a loop point, not on a logo outro.

### Gaps
- No independent 2025-2026 dataset of reach vs. length for Instagram found. Buffer/Later length studies were not retrievable.

## Reels: on-screen text, captions/subtitles, audio

### Takeaway
Design every Reel to work muted. Use burned-in key text (hook and labels) plus Instagram's native auto-captions for speech, and review them. Trending audio is not penalized and is not a direct boost either: Mosseri (2026) says Instagram doesn't boost content just for having music. Original audio and voiceover build a brand sound. Trending audio helps mainly through the audio-page discovery path.

### Cited Findings
- Sound-off figures conflict: the only Instagram-attributed one is "1/3 of videos are played with sound off". The 75-85% figures are vendor claims (one cites an unverified Verizon study) — summary of [OpusClip captions guide](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-caption-subtitle-best-practices) and other vendor results
- Instagram's guidance (via third party): design Reels so they don't rely only on sound — [mediacheatsheet](https://mediacheatsheet-com.pages.dev/blog/how-to-design-for-instagram-reels-a-practical-guide-for-creators-and-marketers/)
- Native auto-captions can be toggled by viewers, which is better for accessibility. Burned-in text is permanent and needs a re-export to fix. Keep the project file / SRT — [OpusClip captions](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-caption-subtitle-best-practices)
- Auto-captions err with music or overlapping speakers, so always review them. Keep text away from the bottom band and right rail, which the UI covers — [Submagic pt-BR](https://submagic.co/pt-br/blog/how-to-add-captions-on-instagram-reels)
- Feed caption truncates after ~125 characters behind "… mais", so the first line must stand alone — [Submagic pt-BR](https://submagic.co/pt-br/blog/how-to-add-captions-on-instagram-reels)
- 2021 policy, still referenced: Instagram deprioritizes "blurry videos covered with text" — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/)
- Trending audio: in a July 2026 AMA, Mosseri reportedly said using popular audio doesn't reduce reach or trigger penalties. In Aug 2026 he reportedly said Instagram doesn't boost content simply because it has music. Any gain comes from engagement and placement eligibility. Paraphrased by Kontentino, not linked to the original — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/trending-audio-instagram-reach/)
- Trending sounds tap existing discovery paths (the audio page). Original audio builds your own discovery path and brand — [Jeff Bullas forum](https://www.jeffbullas.com/thread/does-the-algorithm-prioritize-original-audio-over-trending-sounds-for-reels-disc/); [Chartlex](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/marketing/trending-audio-instagram-reels-algorithm-2026)
- Business accounts: (not verified in this session) commercial accounts often have a restricted music library. Flag for checking in-app.

### Inferences
- Text rules for non-designers:
  - Hook text max ~6-8 words, 2 lines, inside the safe zone (see specs section), upper-middle third.
  - Large bold sans-serif, white with a dark stroke/shadow or on a solid brand-color box.
  - One idea per text card, on screen long enough to read twice (~1 s per 3-4 words).
- Captions: for speech, use Instagram/Edits auto-captions and correct them. For no-speech highlights, burned-in labels only ("Gol aos 87'", "Replay 0.25x").
- Audio: for sports highlights, use the natural sound (ball strike, crowd, whistle) as "original audio", optionally under a trending track at low volume. Voiceover for tutorials.

### Gaps
- No official current font-size spec. No reliable data for burned-in vs. native captions on reach.

## Reels: covers, aspect, resolution, frame rate, safe zones (Reels and Stories)

### Takeaway
Shoot and export 1080×1920, 9:16, 30 fps (60 fps for fast sports action if the source supports it), H.264 MP4. Since Jan 2025 the profile grid is 3:4 (1080×1440), so the cover's key content must sit in the central 1440 px. Reels safe zone per Meta's ad guidance: 14% top / 35% bottom / 6% sides. Stories: keep ~250 px top and ~250-340 px bottom clear.

### Cited Findings
- Specs (Hopper HQ, Sep 2026): 1080×1920, 9:16, min 720 px wide, playback capped at 1080p (4K no benefit), 30 fps min / 60 fps if supported, MP4/MOV, H.264, AAC ≥128 kbps, max 4 GB — [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/)
- Cover: upload at 1080×1920. Grid shows 3:4 (1080×1440), cropping ~240 px top and ~240 px bottom. Grid switched from square to 3:4 in Jan 2025 — [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-swoops-in-with-3-minute-reels-and-rectangular-profile-grids-as-the-tiktok-ban-gets-real-201316339.html)
- "Adjust Preview" lets you pick Fit or Fill per post — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/instagram-extends-reels-to-three-minutes-and-updates-profile-layout-54043.htm)
- Reels safe zones (Meta percentages for Reels ads): top 14% (~270 px), bottom 35% (~670 px), right rail bottom 40% over the rightmost 21% (~230 px wide, ~770 px tall), sides 6% (~65 px). Clean centered area ~950×980 px. Keep 40% bottom clear for ads with disclaimers — [Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/); [Socialync](https://www.socialync.io/tools/safe-zones/instagram-reels)
- Conflicting third-party Reels values: center 1080×1420 (250 px top/bottom) per [Pixotter](https://pixotter.pages.dev/blog/instagram-reel-size/); 220 top / 450 bottom per [Frameo](https://frameo.ai/blog/reel-size/); 450 px bottom per [Adkit](https://adkit.so/tools/safe-zones/instagram)
- Stories: 1080×1920. Top ~250 px (avatar, name, progress bar). Bottom ~250 px (reply bar) for organic posts, or ~340 px (~20%) for ads. Central safe area ~1080×1420. These are community measurements, not a Meta spec — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-story-size); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-story-dimensions-complete-guide); [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/instagram-safe-zone/)
- Link stickers are commonly placed in the lower-middle area, ~200 px above the reply bar — [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/sizes/instagram/stories)

### Inferences
- One template for the team: a 1080×1920 overlay PNG showing 270 px top / 670 px bottom / 65 px left / 230 px right-bottom no-go areas, plus a 1080×1440 dashed box for the grid crop. Key text goes where both "safe" areas overlap: roughly y = 300-1250 px, x = 80-850 px.
- Covers: custom cover with a 3-5 word title in the 3:4 center band, same font and position every time, so the grid reads as a series. Use a face or ball-in-action still, not a blurry mid-motion frame.

### Gaps
- Meta does not publish organic (non-ad) Reels or Stories pixel safe zones. The values above are ad guidance or community measurements. Verify in the app preview on a current iPhone.

## Trial Reels, Edits app, watermarks/originality, collabs

### Takeaway
Use Trial Reels to test hooks on non-followers without touching the grid. Edit in Edits or CapCut, but export clean, without any other app's watermark. Original footage or clearly transformed content matters more after the 2026 aggregator crackdown. Collab Reels are the cheapest way to borrow audiences: brand + athlete/club/creator as co-authors.

### Cited Findings
- Trial Reels (Dec 2024): toggle "Trial" when sharing. Shown only to non-followers, not on the grid or Reels tab. You can publish to followers later if it performs, and Insights compares it with previous trials — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2024/12/13/instagram-launches-trial-reels-so-you-can-test-content-with-non-followers)
- Edits (Instagram's editor, announced Jan 2025, launched 2025): records up to 10 min in 4K, cutout/background removal, effects, text styling, ideas tab, inspiration tab with trending audio, Instagram insights inside the app, draft sharing for feedback, exports without a watermark. Free; premium features hinted — [Digital Camera World](https://www.digitalcameraworld.com/tech/social-media/instagram-finally-lets-users-record-longer-videos-shoot-in-4k-and-skip-watermarks-but-youll-need-this-new-app); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/instagram-is-rolling-out-edits-its-capcut-competitor-163045930.html); [NapoleonCat 2026 walkthrough](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-edits/)
- Claims that Instagram favors Reels made in Edits are unconfirmed — [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/instagrams-edits-app-review)
- Watermarks (2021, still cited): Instagram won't recommend Reels with a TikTok watermark, or any watermark/logo/border, as often — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-will-now-limit-the-reach-of-re-posts-from-tiktok-within-its-reels/594803)
- 2026 aggregator policy (secondary coverage): accounts that mostly repost others' content lose recommendation eligibility. Adding a watermark, speeding up a video, or screenshotting with credit doesn't count as original. "Original" means fully created by you or transformed with a clear added perspective. Reportedly restored after 30 days of original posting — [WERSM](https://wersm.com/instagram-cracks-down-on-content-aggregators-to-boost-originality/); [Techlomedia (Apr 2026)](https://techlomedia.in/2026/04/instagram-cracks-down-on-content-aggregators-pushes-original-creators-123220/)
- Collabs: Later's 2025 benchmark (cited secondhand) reports collab posts +34% engagement, collab Reels +48%. Reach claims of 2-3x come from vendors and are unverified. Co-author limit is inconsistent across sources (3 vs 5). "Collabs 2.0" (late 2025) reportedly adds combined-audience demographics — [Dev.to / Synergist](https://dev.to/synergistdigitalmedia/instagram-collabs-20-what-actually-changed-and-what-marketers-are-getting-wrong-5769); [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-collaboration-posts-guide); [ValueYourNetwork](https://www.valueyournetwork.com/en/instagram-creator-collabs-2026/)
- Context: Metricool data reportedly shows Instagram organic reach down ~31% YoY in 2025 (Reels ~35%) even as posting rose — [Dev.to / Synergist](https://dev.to/synergistdigitalmedia/instagram-collabs-20-what-actually-changed-and-what-marketers-are-getting-wrong-5769)

### Inferences
- For a sports app, user-generated highlights are a risk under the originality rules if the brand account simply reposts them. Repost as a **collab with the player/club** (they're co-author, so it's their original), or transform it with an overlay, voiceover or breakdown ("análise do lance").
- Workflow: post 2-3 hook variants of the same clip as Trial Reels, then publish the winner to followers after 24-72 h.
- Never upload clips exported from CapCut free / TikTok with an end card or logo. Brand logo also small or absent, since "logo/border" was in the de-rank list.

### Gaps
- No Meta primary source for the 2026 aggregator policy or the current co-author limit was retrieved.

## Formats for app/product and sports content (examples from sports apps)

### Takeaway
I found no case studies or data on the Instagram Reels strategy of Strava, Hudl, Veo, PlayHQ, Playtomic, Garmin or WHOOP. The format recommendations below are inferred from the ranking signals and general guidance, and should be validated with Trial Reels.

### Cited Findings
- General guidance: product tutorials tend to work as Reels; behind-the-scenes may get more interactions as carousels; let data decide — summarized from [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/instagram-reels-best-practices.md)
- Recurring series build anticipation — [Cloud Campaign](https://www.cloudcampaign.com/blog/instagram-reels-strategies)
- Hook templates that map to formats: numbered lists ("3 ways to…"), "I wish I knew this before…", mistake hooks — [OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-hook-formulas)
- Original/transformed content is required for recommendations, so reaction/breakdown formats qualify while plain reposts don't — [WERSM](https://wersm.com/instagram-cracks-down-on-content-aggregators-to-boost-originality/)

### Inferences (formats ranked by likely fit for Reprize; not measured)
1. **Lance da semana / user highlights as collab** (shares + sends; tag teammates). Slow-motion replay of the peak moment, text "Repare no…".
2. **POV** ("POV: você fez o gol da vida e alguém gravou") → app shows the clip already cut. Product in context, sendable.
3. **Antes/depois**: messy 40-min phone video vs. the 10-s clip Reprize generated. Clear value demo.
4. **"3 formas de…"** / tutorial: "3 jeitos de nunca perder o melhor lance da pelada". Saves.
5. **Bastidores**: field test, watch tapping to mark a play. Authenticity, founder face (human presence early helps retention per Emplifi summary).
6. **Reação ao lance**: player watching their own replay. Face + emotion in the first second.
7. **"Coisas que eu queria saber"** for amateur players/organizers. Saves.
- Series naming and fixed cover style per format, so the 3:4 grid reads as a menu.

### Gaps
- No brand-specific data from the suggested sports apps. A manual audit of their Instagram profiles would be needed (not done here).

## Stories: frequency, sequence, benchmarks

### Takeaway
Socialinsider's 2025 data (Jan-May 2025) shows exit rates fall as the sequence goes on (23.8% on Story 1 → ~12.5% by Story 15). Reach rate climbs to a peak at about 13 Stories, so sequences of roughly 6-13 frames beat single Stories. Images get higher tap-forward rates than video. Feed posts reach far more followers than Stories for accounts over 5K.

### Cited Findings (all Socialinsider, Jun 2025, ~113k-161k Stories from ~46.5k accounts; internal sample counts inconsistent) — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
- Exit rate by position: #1 23.8%, #2 20.5%, #3 18.5%, #4 15.7%, #9 13.3%, #15 ~12.5%
- Reach rate by sequence length: 1 Story 6.3%, 6 Stories 20.5%, 13 Stories 37.8% (peak). Recommended range: 6-13.
- Tap-forward rate, 1-5K followers: image 56.8%, video 50.5%. 10-50K: image 63.7%, video 56.0%. Video holds slightly better.
- Exit rate, 1-5K followers: image 12.8%, video 13.4%
- Stories per month by tier: 1-5K = 12, 5-10K = 17, 10-50K = 35, 50-100K = 50, 100K-1M = 80. Page also says "at least 6 per day" without data.
- Feed vs. Stories reach rate: 1-5K 4.0% vs 3.3%; 10-50K 3.7% vs 0.6%
- Format mix: ~56% images, ~44% video
- Contrasting data: Dash Social reports ~5% average exit rate and ~70% completion (methodology/year unclear) — [Dash Social](https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/every-instagram-stories-performance-benchmark-you-need-to-know)
- Another 2025 guide says 1-3 Stories/day; 15 s per segment — [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-stories-best-practices)

### Inferences
- Sequence template (7 frames, one daily "episode"):
  1. Hook: a video of the moment, text "Olha o que aconteceu no jogo de ontem".
  2-3. Value: the replay plus the context.
  4. Interaction: poll or quiz ("Foi falta?").
  5. Result/proof: poll results or a screenshot of the app.
  6. Question box: "Manda o seu lance".
  7. CTA: link sticker to download/update.
- Frame 1 carries the biggest exit (~24%), so make it a moving, high-stakes visual, not a text announcement.
- For a new brand account (<5K), Stories reach about 3% of followers, so Stories are a retention/relationship tool, not acquisition. Acquisition happens through Reels.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 data on optimal timing between frames within a day.

## Stories: stickers, DMs, close friends, highlights, Add Yours, link sticker

### Takeaway
No independent study ranks stickers. Practitioner consensus: polls get the most taps (lowest effort), quizzes are next and teach, sliders are medium, and question boxes get fewer taps but the best replies and DM threads. Chaining low- then high-effort stickers works best.

### Cited Findings
- Polls have the highest raw tap rate. Question stickers produce the highest-quality engagement (free text, DM threads) at lower tap rates. Quiz and poll are "medium-strong", slider "medium". Vendor guide, no data — [View-IG-Story blog](https://www.view-ig-story.com/blog/instagram-story-poll-quiz-question-stickers); [SocialRails polls guide](https://socialrails.com/blog/instagram-poll-features-guide)
- Quizzes are underused. "A poll is a preference signal, a quiz is a chance to teach." Viewers must answer to see the correct answer — [Jeff Bullas](https://www.jeffbullas.com/thread/what-are-most-underutilized-instagram-story-stickers-for-boosting-engagement); [Noble Desktop](https://blog.nobledesktop.com/learn/social-media-marketing/creating-polls-and-quizzes-in-instagram-stories-to-boost-engagement)
- Emoji slider fits scale feedback (how much you liked something), not choices between options — [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/10-stickers-that-improve-instagram-stories-engagement/)
- Chaining: quiz → question sticker ("what did you answer first?") deepens the conversation — [Jeff Bullas](https://www.jeffbullas.com/thread/what-are-most-underutilized-instagram-story-stickers-for-boosting-engagement)
- Link sticker placement: lower-middle, ~200 px above the reply bar — [SocialRails](https://socialrails.com/sizes/instagram/stories)

### Inferences
- Sticker ladder for a sports brand: poll ("Gol ou não gol?") → quiz ("Em que minuto saiu o gol?") → slider (🔥 "Quão bonito foi?") → question box ("Manda o vídeo do seu melhor lance"). Answers to the question box become the next Stories (with permission) and DM leads.
- Story-to-DM: reply to every question-box answer by DM. "Responda 'CLIPE' que eu te mando o link" invites DM replies, which the brand then answers personally.
- "Add Yours" template idea: "Meu lance mais bonito de 2026", seeded with the team's own clip. It spreads through followers' networks.
- Highlights: 4-6 fixed covers in the brand palette, e.g. "Como funciona", "Lances da comunidade", "Apple Watch", "Dúvidas", "Novidades". Refresh "Como funciona" with each release.
- Close Friends: for a beta-tester group (early features, field tests). Gives a sense of exclusivity and feedback through replies.

### Gaps
- No data found on Add Yours, countdown, or link sticker tap-through rates, or on Close Friends effects for brands. Mosseri statements on Stories/DMs as a ranking signal were not retrieved in this session. Brazilian sources (mLabs, Rock Content, RD Station) did not surface in search, so local habits remain unverified.

## Accessibility: captions, alt text, contrast

### Takeaway
Use native auto-captions (viewer-toggleable) and review them for errors. Burn in only key text. Add alt text to cover images. Keep high contrast and avoid placing captions where faces, the product or the UI cover them.

### Cited Findings
- Captions include dialogue, sound effects and speaker IDs. Subtitles translate only the dialogue — [OpusClip captions](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-caption-subtitle-best-practices)
- Unedited caption errors reduce accessibility. Don't let captions sit over faces, product demos, usernames or UI — [OpusClip captions](https://www.opus.pro/blog/instagram-reels-caption-subtitle-best-practices)
- Instagram generates automatic alt text via object recognition. Creators can add custom alt text, including on covers — [Combin](https://combin.com/blog/accessible-instagram-content)
- Criticism (opinion): Instagram accessibility is limited, e.g. no zoom or copying text — [Dev.to](https://dev.to/chris_devto/why-accessible-video-is-still-not-possible-on-instagram-8p)

### Inferences
- Contrast rule: white text with a black outline/shadow, or text on a solid box; aim for WCAG 4.5:1 (general web standard, not an Instagram rule). Avoid red/green alone to signal meaning (e.g., "gol/não gol").
- Use descriptive sound labels in sports clips where sound matters: "[torcida grita]", "[apito]".
- Use camel case for hashtags (#LanceDoDia) for screen readers (general accessibility practice; no source retrieved).
