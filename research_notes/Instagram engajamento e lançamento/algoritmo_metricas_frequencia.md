# Instagram ranking, metrics, posting frequency and timing for a new brand account in Brazil (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-08. Many web results are SEO creator blogs that repeat each other. Claims are marked as official (Instagram, Meta or Mosseri), as reported (press coverage quoting Mosseri) or as third-party. Third-party "multipliers" and thresholds are mostly unverified, and the gaps below say so.

## 1. What Instagram and Adam Mosseri have officially said (2025-2026) about ranking signals and penalties

### Takeaway
Officially, ranking predicts how each viewer will respond to a post: whether they watch it, like it, or send it to a friend. The three signals Mosseri names are **watch time, likes per reach and sends (DM shares) per reach**. Likes count slightly more with followers, and sends count slightly more with non-followers. On top of engagement, Instagram values **originality, recency and "breaking" content**. Since 30 Apr 2026, accounts that mostly post unoriginal content in any format (photos, carousels or Reels) lose recommendation eligibility. In 2025-2026 Instagram also added user controls ("Your Algorithm"), a five-hashtag cap, a 3:4 grid, retention and skip-rate metrics, and Reposts.

### Cited Findings
**Signals (Jan 2025, restated through 2026)**
- On 22 Jan 2025 Mosseri said: "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He told creators to "pay close attention to average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach." Likes are "slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." These quotes come via Social Media Today coverage. — [Kompozy summary of Mosseri remarks](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance); [Buffer algorithm guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)
- The widely repeated claim that "sends carry 3-5x the weight of likes" is **not** an official statement. Mosseri said only "slightly more important." Other debunked claims: "original content gets 40-60% more distribution," "skip rate became a ranking signal in July 2026," "first 90 minutes decide reach," fixed watch-time thresholds, and "hashtags boost reach." — [Keywords Everywhere update tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/); [Creatorlane, Mosseri vs measured](https://creatorlanehq.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri-vs-measured)
- Instagram's official creator page lists these recommendation signals: how often and how quickly people like, comment on, share and save a post; the viewer's history with the creator; the viewer's other likes and comments; and viewer controls such as "Not interested." The page dates from 2022 and was updated Apr 2023. — [Instagram Creators: recommendations](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)

**Mosseri's Sept 2026 ranking video series (reported by Social Media Today, 24 Sep 2026)**
- Ranking predicts responses such as watching or sharing with a friend. Recency weighs heavily, and Feed is "roughly in chronological order." Original creators get priority over reposts. Instagram shows posts from **small public creators to non-followers to gauge response**, as a deliberate boost for new creators. Instagram has expanded its AI ranking models. Creators should test content, using **trial Reels** for this, and should watch **engagement rate rather than total views**. On frequency, the advice is to post as much as you can without burning out (paraphrased). Without ranking, the feed would be flooded because "business and brands post a lot more than creators and people." — [Social Media Today, 24 Sep 2026](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)
- Part 2 of the series is quoted as: "Beyond engagement, we also try to value originality, recency, breaking content, and we now have larger AI models to do better at ranking overall." Sources disagree on whether the series had 3 or 4 parts. — [Bazzaal summary](https://www.bazzaal.com/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-mosseri); [Kabo, 3-part reading](https://kabo.sh/guides/instagram-ranking-2026); [Parasocial Magazine](https://www.parasocialmagazine.com/news/mosseri-names-four-instagram-ranking-principles-for-creators/)
- On Meta's Q3 2026 earnings call (21 Oct 2026), Meta said Reels now ranks *sequences* of Reels and that nearly 45% of recommended Reels are under a day old. This comes via a tracker, and I did not read the transcript itself. — [Keywords Everywhere tracker](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)

**Originality, reposts and watermarks**
- **30 Apr 2026 (official):** The originality protections introduced for Reels in 2024 now cover photos and carousels too. "Accounts that mainly post unoriginal content in photo, carousel, and Reels posts will no longer be shown in places where Instagram recommends content." Followers still see them.
  - Original means content you shot or produced, or third-party content you edited significantly (new context text, informative graphics, remix).
  - Re-uploading with only a border, watermark, captions or caption credit does **not** count.
  - Accounts requalify when "a significant share" of their posts over the last 30 days are original. This is computed continuously.
  - To share others' work, use Story shares, Reposts, Collabs or remix. — [Instagram Creators blog, 30 Apr 2026](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram/)
- The 2024 Reels rule (10+ reposts of others' content in 30 days leads to exclusion from recommendations) is third-party reporting. The 2026 policy publishes no numeric threshold. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/); [Coruzant](https://coruzant.com/analytics/instagram-algorithm-2026-guide/)
- The official Reels guidance for recommendations reads:
  - high-resolution 9:16 vertical, full screen, no borders
  - text must not cover most of the frame
  - "To be recommended, your content must be 3 minutes or less" (2023 text, written before 3-min Reels; see Gaps)
  - hook in the first 3 seconds
  - avoid "visibly recycled" content: watermarks, blur, low quality
  - for Search, use keywords in username, name and bio, and put keywords and hashtags in the caption, not comments — [Instagram Creators: recommendations](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- Reels carrying TikTok or other app watermarks being less discoverable is an older policy (Feb 2021). — [Tubefilter 2021](https://www.tubefilter.com/2021/02/10/instagram-reels-with-tiktok-watermark-less-discoverable/amp/)

**Product and platform changes, 2025-2026 (dated)**
- **Jan 2025:** The profile grid changed from 1:1 to a taller 3:4 crop. Mosseri said most uploads are vertical and that square crops were "pretty brutal." Users can't revert, but "Adjust preview" sets the crop. The upload maximum stays 1080x1350 (4:5). — [Business Today, 21 Jan 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21); [Kapwing grid guide](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagrams-new-grid-layout-size-and-dimensions-2025/)
- **Jan 2025:** Reels length extended to 3 minutes, announced in the same Mosseri update. — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21)
- **6 Aug 2025:** Reposts launched globally and now appear in feed recommendations. — [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/08/new-instagram-features-help-you-connect/)
- **Aug 2025:** Reels Insights added a Retention chart and **Skip rate**, the share of viewers who scroll away in the first 3 seconds. Skip rate replaced the old "view rate." — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992); [Metricool reel analytics](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)
- **10 Dec 2025:** "Your Algorithm" launched on Reels. Users see the topics inferred for them and can add or remove them. It extended to Explore (16 Apr 2026) and to the main Feed (10 Jun 2026). — [Instagram blog](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/reels-algorithm-control); [Social Media Today, Feed extension](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-extends-your-algorithm-to-the-main-feed/822576/)
- **18 Dec 2025:** Hashtags capped at **5 per post**. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/)
- **31 Aug 2026:** Unlabeled AI-generated profiles lose recommendation eligibility. — [Instagram Creators blog](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/ai-generated-profile-label/)
- **10 Jul 2025:** Public professional content became indexable by Google by default. This is third-party reporting. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- Trial Reels, which are shown to non-followers first, launched 10 Dec 2024 (outside the 2025-26 window). Mosseri reportedly said trials "will get less reach, almost always" than a regular Reel. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/); [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/why-trial-reels-get-no-views)

### Inferences
- These signals can be turned into rules: optimize the first 3 seconds (skip rate), total retention (watch time), and "would someone DM this to a friend?" (sends per reach). Normalize everything by reach, not followers.
- A brand repurposing user clips must add real editorial value (context text, graphics, its own footage). Otherwise the whole account risks losing non-follower distribution. Never upload with another app's watermark.
- Format specs: vertical 9:16 for Reels; 3:4 or 4:5 for feed images so the grid crop doesn't cut key content; 5 hashtags or fewer; keywords in name, bio and caption.

### Gaps
- No official numeric weights for any signal.
- The official page's "3 minutes or less to be recommended" line predates the Jan 2025 extension to 3-minute Reels. I found no official update on whether Reels longer than 3 min (if allowed) are recommended.
- I found no fetched source for the Edits app launch (Meta's standalone video editor, reportedly April 2025). Whether using Edits affects ranking is unconfirmed, and I found no claim that it does.
- Text-heavy penalty: officially, the only guidance is that text shouldn't "cover most of the screen." There is no quantified penalty.

## 2. Account-level and content-level eligibility for recommendations (Account Status, Recommendation Guidelines)

### Takeaway
Instagram uses two tiers. The **Community Guidelines** decide what can exist on the platform. The stricter **Recommendation Guidelines** decide what can be shown to non-followers in Explore, Reels, Feed suggestions, Search and Suggested Accounts. A single post can be withheld from recommendations. Repeated violations, a violating profile photo or bio, AI profiles without a label, or mostly unoriginal posting can make the **whole account** ineligible. Professional accounts can check this in **Account Status**.

### Cited Findings
- Recommendations are posts and accounts shown to non-followers in Explore, Reels and Feed recommendations. Since Apr 2023, Account Status also covers Search and Suggested accounts. Only public accounts are recommended, and business and creator accounts are public by default. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- Recommendation Guidelines are "designed to maintain a higher standard than our Community Guidelines." They list types of content that are allowed but may not be recommended, including sensitive or low-quality health or finance content. "If an account repeatedly posts content that violates our guidelines, the account may be ineligible for recommendation for a period of time." — [Instagram Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232)
- Content that may not be recommended includes violence (such as fights), regulated products (tobacco, vaping), and sexually explicit or suggestive content. A profile photo or bio that violates the rules can make the account ineligible. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- Account Status lives at Settings > Account > Account Status. It shows whether content can be recommended, sample violating content or profile elements, and removed content. You can edit or delete a post or request a review. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators); [TechCrunch, Dec 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/12/07/instagrams-new-transparency-tools-will-tell-you-if-your-content-is-ineligible-to-be-recommended/)
- Unoriginality ineligibility (since 30 Apr 2026) is evaluated on a rolling 30-day window and can be appealed in Account Status. — [Instagram Creators, originality](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/rewarding-original-creators-on-instagram/)
- A third-party source says ineligible accounts also lose access to Trial Reels, and that trial Reels need 200+ followers for professional accounts. This is unverified against Instagram's help pages. — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/why-trial-reels-get-no-views)
- Instagram has announced no "shadowban" system. The announced mechanism is recommendation eligibility, shown in Account Status. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)

### Inferences
- Rule: check Account Status weekly, or after any reach drop. Treat "not eligible" as a P0 incident: find the post, remove it or appeal.
- For a sports-clip app brand, watch the "violence (fights)" category. Clips of on-field fights could be withheld from recommendations even though they're allowed.

### Gaps
- Instagram doesn't publish how long account-level ineligibility lasts, or what "significant share" of original posts means in numbers.

## 3. Benchmarks: engagement rate by format, reach by format, carousels vs Reels (small accounts)

### Takeaway
Brand engagement rates are low and falling. Socialinsider puts the 2025 per-follower averages at **carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%, images 0.37%**, and Q3 2026 at 0.50% / 0.48% / 0.35%. Rival IQ's 2025 median across brands is about **0.36%**, with about 1% as the top-quartile cutoff. Carousels lead on engagement and saves. Reels lead on comments and shares and are the main route to non-followers. For a small new account, per-reach metrics matter more than per-follower rates.

### Cited Findings
- **Socialinsider (per follower: (likes + comments) / followers; about 579K business accounts with 1K-1M followers):**
  - All formats: 0.50% (2024), 0.48% (2025), 0.45% (Q3 2026)
  - Reels: 0.50% → 0.52% → 0.48%
  - Carousels: 0.55% → 0.55% → 0.50%
  - Images: 0.45% → 0.37% → 0.35% — [Socialinsider Instagram benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Socialinsider's 2025 PDF gives Reels 0.50% for 2025, which conflicts with the web page's 0.52%. The page's "-24% YoY" headline doesn't match its own table. — [Socialinsider 2025 PDF](https://cdn.socialinsider.io/documents/instagram_benchmarks_2025.pdf); [Socialinsider page](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- **Small-account absolutes, 1-5K followers (Socialinsider, 2025):**

  | Format | Avg views/post | Avg comments/post | Avg saves/post |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Reels | 580 | 3 | 1 |
  | Carousels | 993 | 2 | 1 |
  | Images | 417 | 1 | 1 |

  - At 5-10K followers: Reels 1,000 views, carousels 2,117, images 1,068. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Average yearly follower growth for 1-5K accounts fell from 38% (2024) to 22% (2025). — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Socialinsider's engagement report (15M posts): Reels have the highest median comments and shares, and carousels the highest median saves. — [Socialinsider engagement report](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-engagement-report)
- **Rival IQ 2025:** median about 0.36% per follower across brands; top 25% at about 1.02-1.05% or more (sources disagree); spread from about 0.1% to 2.1% by industry (Higher Ed 2.10%, Food & Bev 0.40%, Travel 0.34%). Rival IQ counts likes, comments, shares and reactions but not saves. — [Inmagic summary of Rival IQ](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-engagement-rate-benchmarks/); [Rival IQ](https://rivaliq.com/?p=35627)
- **Metricool 2026 (global, 24.4M posts):** Reels average reach rate 37.87%; average watch time up 117% vs 2025; about 70% of a Reel's views come in its first 3 days and about 30% on day 1. — [Metricool PT](https://metricool.com/pt/melhor-horario-para-postar-instagram/)
- **Brazil, HypeAuditor x Metricool** (3.5M Brazilian creator accounts, 83M posts, Jun 2025-Jun 2026):
  - Carousels are 14-27% of posts and can match or beat Reels on reach and likes, especially above 1M followers.
  - Reels are the most-used format.
  - Stories reach in Brazil is below the global average, but viewers who start a Story sequence drop off less.
  - 80% of Brazilian creator accounts are nano (≤10K followers).
  - Audience: 43% aged 25-34, 61% of reach is women.
  - The article publishes no per-format numbers. — [Propmark, Sep 2026](https://propmark.com.br/digital/reels-ou-carrossel-qual-gera-mais-engajamento-no-instagram-em-2026/)
- **Stories (Socialinsider 2025):**
  - Exit rate is 23.8% on the 1st Story, 20.5% on the 2nd, 18.5% on the 3rd, and about 12.5% by Story 15.
  - Exit rates rose vs 2024.
  - Best reach growth comes at 6-13 Stories per day.
  - Video Stories get higher reach.
  - Tap-forward at 1-5K followers is about 57% for images vs about 50% for video (read from the table layout).
  - Dash Social cites about 70% completion (68.3% for accounts up to 190K), but its own exit figures look inconsistent. — [Socialinsider Stories benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks); [Dash Social](https://www.dashsocial.com/blog/every-instagram-stories-performance-benchmark-you-need-to-know)
- Buffer notes that Stories engage existing followers but don't drive growth, since non-followers typically don't see them. — [Buffer frequency study](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)

### Inferences
- A sensible format mix for growth: Reels for reach to non-followers and shares; carousels for saves and engagement and to reach followers; few single images; Stories for retention and link clicks, not for discovery.
- Per-follower benchmarks (0.4-0.6%) are misleading for accounts under 1K followers, where non-follower reach dominates. Use per-reach ratios internally (see section 5).

### Gaps
- No reliable public benchmark for **per-reach** engagement, sends per reach, or saves per reach for small accounts. mLabs Índice has real-reach engagement for Brazil by segment and follower range, but the numbers sit behind an interactive tool and aren't in the article. — [mLabs Índice](https://www.mlabs.com.br/blog/mlabs-indice)
- No accounts under 1K in the Socialinsider sample. I found no per-format reach-rate table from Socialinsider. Hootsuite, Later and Emplifi 2025-26 format figures were not retrieved.

## 4. Posting frequency and best times/days in Brazil (BRT)

### Takeaway
More posting brings more reach and growth, with diminishing returns. Buffer's within-account study (2.1M posts) recommends **3-5 feed posts per week**. Moving from 1-2 to 3-5 per week gives about 12% more reach per post and about 2x the follower growth. The Brazil data agrees: 90% of Brazilian profiles post once a week or less and lose followers, while those posting 2-3 per week grow. For timing, the Brazilian peak is the **evening, 20h-00h**, and global Metricool data puts the best window at 18h-21h with a peak at 20h. Every Brazilian source warns that the peak is also the most crowded slot and says to use your own Insights.

### Cited Findings
- **Buffer (13 Aug 2025, about 2.1M feed posts from about 102K accounts, each compared with its own baseline; Stories excluded):**

  | Posts per week | Median reach per post vs 1-2 | Weekly follower growth rate |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1-2 | baseline | +0.12% |
  | 3-5 | about +12% | +0.26% |
  | 6-9 | about +18% | +0.44% |
  | 10+ | about +24% | +0.66% |

  - Recommended: 3-5 per week. 6-9+ maximizes growth, but treat it "with caution" because of quality and burnout risk.
  - The sample is Buffer's own users, and there's no breakdown by format or account size. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
- **Brazil (HypeAuditor x Metricool, 2025-26):** About 90% of Brazilian profiles post ≤1 per week and lose about 2% of followers per year. Profiles posting 2-3 per week grow 26%, and those posting more than 10 per week grow 93% (the period isn't stated). — [Propmark](https://propmark.com.br/digital/reels-ou-carrossel-qual-gera-mais-engajamento-no-instagram-em-2026/)
- Socialinsider's average brand cadence in 2025: 8 Reels, 5 carousels and 7 images per month, about 20 per month or about 5 per week. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Mosseri (Sept 2026, paraphrased): post as much as you can to raise the odds of a hit while avoiding burnout. Recency weighs heavily. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)
- **Brazil timing, mLabs / Kiso Insights (28 Feb 2026):**
  - 27% of Brazilians access Instagram between 20h and 00h, the peak window.
  - Almost half of Reels consumption happens at night.
  - 64% watch Reels daily (40% several times a day).
  - The article recommends no fixed time: the peak means more competition, so match format to time of day (night: exploratory; afternoon: quick and clear; morning: functional) and use your own history.
  - The underlying survey isn't named. Nielsen is cited for 3-4 exposures per week per person, with no date. — [mLabs](https://www.mlabs.com.br/blog/melhores-horarios-para-postar-nas-redes-sociais/)
- **Metricool 2026 (global, 24.4M posts, 375K accounts; timezone not stated, presumably local):**
  - Best overall 18h-21h, peak 20h; worst 2h-5h.
  - Best hour is 20h every day except Thursday (18h); Tuesday 20h is the top slot of the week.
  - Reels: Sunday has the best average views; midnight posts average the most views but at low volume; 18h is the most crowded hour, with lower average views.
  - Weaker, less clearly attributed suggestions: Stories 11h-14h, carousels Sunday 19h-21h. — [Metricool PT](https://metricool.com/pt/melhor-horario-para-postar-instagram/)
- **Other Brazilian guides (lower quality, little or no stated data):**
  - Stone 2025: weekday evenings, Reels around 17h-18h — [Stone](https://conteudo.stone.com.br/melhor-horario-para-postar-no-instagram/)
  - IBTA: 11h-13h and 18h-20h on weekdays, best on Tuesday and Thursday — [IBTA](https://ibta.com.br/melhores-horarios-para-postar-no-instagram/)
  - Amper: 20h-00h peak, but no universal time — [Amper](https://www.amper.ag/post/melhor-horario-postar-instagram-brasil)
  - Neil Patel BR: Tuesday to Thursday — [Neil Patel BR](https://neilpatel.com/br/blog/qual-o-melhor-horario-para-postar-no-instagram-descubra-as-melhores-estrategias/)
  - RD Station's page appears older and wasn't verified. — [RD Station](https://www.rdstation.com/blog/marketing/melhores-horarios-para-postar-no-instagram/)
- mLabs Índice (Dec 2025, more than 30M posts scheduled via mLabs) shows the most-used posting hours and real engagement by Brazilian segment, but only in its interactive tool. — [mLabs Índice](https://www.mlabs.com.br/blog/mlabs-indice)

### Inferences
- Starting rule for a new account: 4-5 feed posts per week (for example 3 Reels and 2 carousels), plus Stories on most days. Raise volume only if quality holds.
- Starting time slots (BRT): weekday evenings 18h-21h. Test 12h-13h (lunch) and Sunday evening. Alternate slots for 4-6 weeks, then switch to the "Most active times" in your own Insights once you have about 100+ followers.
- Because about 70% of Reel views arrive within 3 days, the posting hour matters less for Reels than for Feed posts. Recency matters, but the "first hour" myth is unsupported.
- For a sports or amateur-football audience (a guess about this app's audience), post-match evening and weekend timing is plausible but untested. Validate it with the app's own analytics.

### Gaps
- No primary Brazil-only dataset publishes hour-by-hour Instagram posting performance. mLabs, Etus and Reportei data are tool-gated or not found, and I found no Etus or Reportei 2025-26 study.
- No frequency study separates Reels from carousels, or covers accounts under 1K followers.

## 5. Which Insights metrics to track weekly and simple targets for a new account

### Takeaway
Track per-reach ratios, not raw counts. Track Reels by **skip rate, average watch time and retention**. Watch **non-follower reach share** (is the account being recommended?), **sends** and **saves**, and the funnel from profile visits to follows and link taps. Mosseri explicitly tells creators to watch average watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach, and engagement rate rather than total views.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri: watch "average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach" (Jan 2025). Focus on engagement rate, not total views (Sept 2026). — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance); [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-shares-algorithm-insights-and-posting-tips/831326/)
- Definitions: likes per reach = likes / accounts reached; sends per reach = DM shares / accounts reached. — [Kompozy](https://kompozy.io/news/instagram-mosseri-ranking-signals-guidance)
- Since Aug 2024, Views is the primary metric across Reels, Stories, photos and carousels. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/topical-spot/instagram-new-engagement-tools-explained-9762802)
- Skip rate is the percentage of viewers who skip within the first 3 seconds. The Retention chart shows where viewers drop off (Aug 2025). The API returns skip rate only above an undisclosed view threshold. — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-retention-chart-skip-rate-new-performance-metrics-reels-9730992); [Storrito API notes](https://storrito.com/resources/how-instagram-marketing-api-metrics-work/)
- A high skip rate combined with a low share rate suggests a weak hook or an unclear topic (third-party interpretation). — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reel-analytics/)
- mLabs defines "real engagement" as (likes + comments + shares + saves) / reach. — [mLabs Índice](https://www.mlabs.com.br/blog/mlabs-indice)
- Story benchmarks for targets: first-Story exit about 24%, settling at about 12-18%; image tap-forward about 57% at 1-5K followers. — [Socialinsider Stories](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram-stories-benchmarks)
- Reels average reach rate is about 38% (Metricool, global). — [Metricool PT](https://metricool.com/pt/melhor-horario-para-postar-instagram/)

### Inferences (proposed weekly dashboard; targets are heuristics, not sourced benchmarks)
- Weekly KPIs:
  - accounts reached (and % non-followers)
  - views
  - engagement per reach
  - shares (sends) per reach
  - saves per reach
  - profile visits per reach
  - follows per profile visit
  - link taps (bio + sticker)
  - Reels: skip rate, average watch time, % watched to the end (retention chart)
  - Stories: reach per follower, exit rate on Story 1, taps forward, link-sticker taps
  - Account Status: eligible yes/no
- Directional targets for a small account, to calibrate after 4-6 weeks of data:
  - non-follower reach on Reels should be the majority. Unverified blogs claim 30-60%, so treat that as a hypothesis.
  - skip rate should trend down week over week.
  - engagement per reach should beat the per-follower averages above (0.5%).
  - first-Story exit should stay under about 24%.
- Compare each post with the account's own trailing median, the way Buffer's within-account method does, rather than with industry averages.

### Gaps
- I found no official or robust third-party "good" values for skip rate, average watch time, sends per reach or non-follower %. Any targets will need the account's own baseline.

## 6. Business vs Creator account for an app brand; link limits and link stickers

### Takeaway
For a company or app brand, a **Business** account is the natural fit. It supports Shops and product tagging, location, and integrates with Meta Business tools and scheduling tools. Creator accounts are aimed at individuals and get creator monetization tools such as affiliate links, branded content marketplace and subscriptions. Recommendation eligibility, Account Status and Insights are available to both professional types. Both can add **up to 5 links in bio** (since Apr 2023), and Story **link stickers** are reportedly open to all accounts.

### Cited Findings
- Business accounts are for companies, with Shops, product tags and location. Creator accounts are for personal brands, with brand-collab, affiliate and subscription tools. The 2025 differences are "minor," and full Instagram Shops remain limited to business profiles. — [NapoleonCat](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-creator-vs-business-account/); [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-creator-vs-business)
- Only public accounts are recommended, and business and creator accounts are public by default. Account Status is available to professional accounts. — [Instagram Creators](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-recommendations-eligibility-tips-creators)
- Up to 5 bio links for all accounts, added via Edit profile > Links (April 2023). Only the first link shows inline, and the rest sit behind a tap. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/instagram-now-allows-up-to-5-links-in-bio-395742); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2023/04/18/instagram-multiple-links-in-bio/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/instagram-lets-you-add-five-links-to-your-profile-so-you-can-stop-using-linktree-161957490.html)
- Link stickers in Stories replaced swipe-up in 2021 and are said to be available regardless of follower count. This comes from SEO guides, not an official page. — [inmagic link guide](https://inmagic.ai/instagram-link-in-bio/)
- Sep 2026: the Meta One subscription allows clickable links in posts and Reels (4, 8 or 12 a month depending on plan). Facebook's two-link test doesn't apply to Instagram. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)
- Jun 2026: Instagram Plus ($3.99) launched with Story Spotlight. — [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com/news/instagram-algorithm-updates/)

### Inferences
- Rule: use a Business account. Bio link 1 should be the App Store/Play deep link or a smart link with UTM. Use links 2-5 for the site and support. Add a link sticker to every launch or feature Story, and measure taps.
- Third-party scheduling and analytics tools (mLabs, Metricool, Buffer) rely on the professional-account API, which works with either professional type.

### Gaps
- I found no official Help Center page in 2025-26 confirming link-sticker eligibility for all accounts or current Business-only features. I also found no evidence that account type affects ranking, and no source claims it does.
