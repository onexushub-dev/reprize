# Instagram feed in 2025-2026: carousels, single images, grid, captions, hashtags, SEO and design patterns

Research date: 2026-11-08. Sources are mostly 2025-2026. Most of them are vendor or marketing blogs (Socialinsider, Buffer, Metricool, Planoly and others). Primary Instagram/Meta statements appear only through press coverage of Mosseri's posts, and I flag where that is the case. Older data (2023-2024) is labeled.

## 1. Carousels: slide count, hooks, the second-slide re-serve, video mixing, data vs other formats

### Takeaway
Carousels are still the top feed format for engagement and views per post among brand accounts in 2025, ahead of Reels and well ahead of single images. Reels still win on raw reach in Buffer's data. The limit is 20 slides (since Aug 2024). The only slide-count data is from the 10-slide era: more slides did better, and engagement rose again at 8+. Instagram re-serves unswiped carousels starting at slide 2, so slide 2 must work as a second cover. Mixing images and video did best in the older Socialinsider study.

### Cited Findings
**Platform facts**
- Carousels were expanded to up to 20 photos/videos per post in August 2024 (tested at 15 in March 2024, previously 10 since 2017). Any count from 1 to 20 is allowed, and images and videos can be mixed — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-expands-carousels-to-20-frames/723792/); [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2024/08/09/instagram-users-can-now-share-20-photos-videos-in-a-post-carousel-photodump); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-increases-carousel-content-limit/). I found no later change to the 20 cap.
- **New in 2026: per-slide captions.** Instagram is gradually rolling out an optional choice of one caption for the whole carousel or a separate caption on each slide (up to 20). The per-slide caption shows at the bottom of the screen as the viewer swipes. Brazilian press dates it to June-July 2026 — [Metricool PT](https://metricool.com/pt/legendas-carrossel-instagram/); [Olhar Digital, 2026-06-18](https://olhardigital.com.br/2026/06/18/internet-e-redes-sociais/instagram-libera-legendas-diferentes-para-cada-foto-do-carrossel-veja-como-usar/); [O Povo, 2026-07-31](https://www.opovo.com.br/noticias/tecnologia/2026/07/31/instagram-agora-permite-legendas-diferentes-no-carrossel.html). Metricool recommends it for tutorials and step-by-step content, and for accessibility. One guide says to use per-slide captions when each slide needs its own explanation and a single caption when the carousel makes one point — [Creatorsjet](https://www.creatorsjet.com/pt-BR/blog/multiplas-legendas-instagram-carrosseis). Sources say the main caption still carries the intro, hashtags and CTA — [Seu Crédito Digital](https://seucreditodigital.com.br/instagram-legendas-diferentes/). Unknown: whether per-slide captions are indexed for search.

**Second-slide re-serve (official)**
- Mosseri, in an Instagram Reel: "If someone sees your carousel post but they don't swipe, we'll often give that carousel a second chance and automatically move to that second piece of media for the viewer." This is reported secondhand in [ccstartup](https://ccstartup.com/blog/2024/10/21/why-instagram-carousels-sometimes-start-on-the-second-slide/) and [Socialinsider Carousel Study PDF](https://www.socialinsider.io/data-geeks/Instagram-Carousel-Study.pdf). The PDF says it happens once, typically showing the second photo/video. The thresholds are not published.
- Vendor advice that follows from this: make slide 2 a standalone hook, front-load the two strongest slides, and make every slide worth saving or sending on its own — [Social Boost Digital](https://socialboostdigital.com/blog/instagram-carousel-algorithm-double-exposure); [PostNitro](https://postnitro.ai/blog/post/carousel-swipe-through-rate-optimization).

**Slide count (older data, 10-slide era)**
- Socialinsider studied about 3M carousels. **10-slide carousels had the highest engagement rate.** Engagement dips after 3 slides and rises again at 8+. Most brands post 2-4 slides, and only 6% used all 10 — [Search Engine Journal on Socialinsider](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-carousels/379311/). This is pre-2024 data from before the 20-slide limit. I found no study of 11-20 slides.
- Socialinsider (Feb 2026 page) gives no slide-count data. It only says to make carousels longer if each slide adds value — [Socialinsider 2026 benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-posts-study/).

**Mixed media**
- Carousels mixing images and videos had the highest engagement per post (2.33%) in the older Socialinsider study — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/instagram-carousels/379311/). This is pre-2025. I found no 2025-2026 replication.

**Format performance, 2025**
- Socialinsider, 67.5M organic posts from about 579K brand accounts (1K-1M followers), published 2026-02-20. Engagement rate by followers in 2025: **Carousel 0.55%** (flat vs 2024), Reels 0.52% (from 0.50%), Images 0.37% (from 0.45%). The overall average is 0.48%. Q3 2026 carousels averaged 0.50%, still the highest format — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-posts-study/). Caveat: the page's dates are inconsistent (its methodology says Jan 2024-Dec 2026). Other pages cite 35M posts / 447K accounts for an earlier edition.
- Same source, average views per post in 2025 for 1-5K follower brands: **carousel 993, Reels 580, images 417**. For 5-10K: 2,117 / 1,000 / 1,068. Saves at 10-50K: carousel 8, Reels 7, images 3. Reels earned the most comments in every size band — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-posts-study/).
- Socialinsider's reach rate (Jan 2025-Aug 2026): carousels 4.50%, Reels 4.10%, single images 4.00% — reported in search summaries of [Socialinsider benchmarks](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-benchmark-study/). I did not verify this on the page.
- **Conflict:** Buffer (4M+ posts, Jan 2022-Oct 2024) found Reels reach about 36% more than carousels, while carousels lead on engagement by about 12% — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-reach-engagement-analysis/). These figures come via secondary write-ups, and the data is older. Both sources agree carousels win on engagement. They disagree on reach, probably because Socialinsider counts carousel views, which include the re-serves.
- Metricool says carousels outperform single images on reach, saves and shares, yet are published less often — [Metricool PT](https://metricool.com/pt/legendas-carrossel-instagram/).
- A smaller study (ProflUp, 11.2K posts) found carousels beat Reels by up to 35% in interaction rate in education, wellness and travel — [IPS News](https://ipsnews.net/business/?p=230206). This is a vendor press release, so it is low confidence.

**Ranking signals that matter for carousels**
- Mosseri (Jan 2025) named watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as top signals. Likes weigh slightly more with followers and sends weigh slightly more with non-followers — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/). The order among the three is not official.

**Educational vs storytelling**
- Guides suggest starting with one carousel per week in an educational listicle format, plus brand-humanizing behind-the-scenes and story content — [Creatorflow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-carousel-posts-guide); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-to-create-engaging-instagram-carousels/522723/). I found no head-to-head data on educational vs storytelling carousels.

### Inferences
- Default carousel for a small app team: **7-10 slides**. Go to 12-15 only for true step-by-step tutorials where every slide carries one idea. Data beyond 10 slides does not exist, so test it.
- Template (educational, 8 slides):
  1. Cover: a benefit or curiosity hook in 5-9 words, big type, a real product or real-use photo, and a small swipe cue (an arrow, or a cut-off element bleeding into slide 2).
  2. A second cover that restates the hook from a new angle ("O problema: ..."), readable cold, because it is the re-serve slide.
  3. to 6. One point per slide, max about 25-30 words, with a numbered header.
  7. Proof or an example (a screenshot or a clip from the app; a video slide here mixes media).
  8. CTA slide: "Salve para o próximo jogo" or "Envie para quem joga com você", plus the brand mark.
- Storytelling variant: cover with a question or tension, then 5-8 slides of narrative in chronological order with real photos, ending on the product moment and the CTA.
- Use per-slide captions for tutorials (an accessibility gain). Keep the main caption for the hook, keywords, hashtags and CTA.
- Optimize for sends and saves: checklists, how-tos, "erros comuns" and comparison tables are naturally saveable and sendable.

### Gaps
- No data on 11-20 slide carousels, and no 2025-2026 data on video-in-carousel performance.
- The thresholds for the re-serve (how often, and whether it happens for non-followers or only followers) are not published.
- Whether per-slide captions are indexed by Instagram search or Google is unknown.
- I found no rigorous data on swipe-cue effectiveness (arrows vs bleed elements). The advice is practitioner opinion only.

## 2. Image specs and grid: 4:5 vs 3:4, designing for feed and grid, pinned posts

### Takeaway
Since January 2025 the profile grid shows 3:4 tiles, and since late May 2025 the feed accepts native 3:4 uploads (1080x1440). Post at 1080x1440 (3:4) so the feed shows the full image and the grid shows it uncropped. If you must post 4:5 (1080x1350), keep critical content in the central 1012x1350 area. Pin up to 3 posts as an evergreen "start here" row.

### Cited Findings
- The profile grid changed from 1:1 squares to 3:4 rectangles in January 2025, and users cannot revert it — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-testing-vertical-profile-display/); [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagrams-new-grid-layout-size-and-dimensions-2025/); [Hopper HQ](https://help.hopperhq.com/en/articles/10907941-instagram-s-vertical-grid). Mosseri's rationale was that most uploads are vertical and square crops were too harsh.
- Around 2025-05-29, Mosseri announced on Threads that Instagram supports full 3:4 photo uploads without cropping, for single photos and carousels, on iOS and Android, because 3:4 is the default on most phone cameras. Square and 4:5 still work — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/05/29/instagram-finally-adds-support-for-34-aspect-ratio-photos); [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/2025/05/30/instagram-announces-support-for-the-most-commonly-used-photo-aspect-ratio); [India TV](https://www.indiatvnews.com/technology/news/instagram-finally-adds-native-3-4-aspect-ratio-for-photos-for-ios-and-android-2025-05-31-992687).
- 1080x1440 (3:4) fills the grid tile exactly with no crop. A 4:5 (1080x1350) post loses a thin strip on each side in the grid (the visible area is about 1012-1015x1350). Square posts are center-cropped in the grid — [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagrams-new-grid-layout-size-and-dimensions-2025/); [Neal Schaffer](https://nealschaffer.com/instagram-post-size/); [Dream Pixel Forge](https://www.dreampixelforge.com/blog/instagram-post-size). Pixel figures for the 4:5 safe area vary slightly between blogs.
- The grid thumbnail of a carousel is its first slide, so the cover doubles as the grid tile (standard platform behavior, consistent across sizing guides above).
- Pinned posts: up to 3 posts, Reels or carousels can be pinned to the top of the grid, shown with a pin icon. One blog says the order follows pin order (newest pinned on the left) — [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/instagram-marketing-how-to-pin-posts-and-reels-to-a-profile/); [SocialKit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/instagram-pinned-posts-strategy). The ordering detail is single-source.
- Pinning advice: choose representative evergreen content (intro or "what we are", a best tutorial, social proof) rather than the most viral post. Review the pins monthly so stale promos do not hurt credibility (the monthly review is attributed to Sprout Social) — [Unum](https://www.unum.la/blog/how-to-strategically-pin-instagram-posts-and-reels); [SocialKit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/instagram-pinned-posts-strategy).
- Consistent colors, fonts and layout across slides give a cohesive look — [Adaptly](https://adaptlypost.com/pt/blog/text-overlays-on-instagram-carousels).

### Inferences
- **Canvas standard: 1080x1440 for everything in the feed**, including carousel slides. It matches the grid, so there is no crop to design around. Keep text inside about a 60-80px margin, and keep important elements out of the bottom of the frame, where per-slide captions now overlay (exact overlay height unknown).
- Reels covers should also be designed to the 3:4 grid crop of the 9:16 frame (the central area). Verify this in the app before publishing.
- Grid planning: every first slide is a grid tile. Use a recognizable system (for example, a consistent type position, one or two brand background colors, and an alternating photo/graphic rhythm). Preview a 3-column, 3-4 row layout in Canva/Figma or a planner before publishing.
- Pinned trio for a launching app: (1) "O que é a Reprize" explainer carousel or Reel, (2) the best how-to or tutorial, (3) proof (a field test, user clip, or reviews). Revisit monthly.

### Gaps
- I found no official Instagram Help Center page with exact safe-area pixels, so the figures come from third-party guides.
- I found no data on whether 3:4 posts outperform 4:5 in reach or engagement. Taller posts take more screen space, but this is not measured.
- Highlights: one 2026 blog claims you can feature 3 highlight covers above the grid, but this is unverified.

## 3. Captions: length, first-line hook, CTAs, comment-to-DM, emojis, line breaks

### Takeaway
Mosseri said in Aug 2025 that caption length barely affects reach. Vendor data from 2023 favors short captions for brands, and academic work finds an inverted U. The practical rule: a strong first line (it is what shows before "mais"), then either a short body or a structured longer one with keywords. Comment-to-DM keyword automation is allowed only through Meta's official API and within the 24-hour messaging window.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri, in a Stories Q&A, on whether caption length affects reach: "It's not going to affect your reach much one way or the other." Long captions are optional and carry no penalty — [Social Media Today, 2025-08-24](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-chief-says-longer-captions-dont-impact-post-reach/758462/).
- Socialinsider (9.1M posts, Jan-Jul 2023): captions of up to 10 words gave the highest business engagement. Carousel engagement fell to about 0.70% with more than 10 words. A later Socialinsider page says 90+ words was the weakest range across Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn — [Socialinsider caption length](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-caption-length/); [Socialinsider best practices](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/social-media-caption-length-best-practices/). This is older (2023) and correlational.
- **Conflict:** A preregistered academic study of about 75K posts from about 1,000 influencers found a non-linear (inverted-U) effect of text length on engagement — [EMAC 2025 proceedings](https://proceedings.emac-online.org/pdfs/A2025-126401.pdf).
- Caption limit: 2,200 characters (per Meta documentation, cited by [Adaptly](https://adaptlypost.com/pt/blog/instagram-carousel-limit)).
- Instagram now weighs words in captions, bio language, alt text keywords and on-image text for search and discovery — [Phable](https://www.phable.io/phable-labs/instagram-hashtags-keywords-discovery); [That Eric Alper](https://www.thatericalper.com/?p=238828). These are secondary sources.
- Comment-to-DM ("comente PALAVRA e receba o link"): permitted when run through Meta's official API (for example ManyChat, a Meta Business Partner). Messaging is constrained by Meta's 24-hour window, and cold outreach is not possible. Risk comes from tools that ask for your password or automate the app — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/is-comment-to-dm-allowed-on-instagram); [ManyChat rules](https://manychat.com/blog/instagram-dm-automation-rules/); [Kanal](https://getkanal.com/blog/manychat-instagram). Delivery is not guaranteed. One user reported link DMs stopping after applying for Meta monetization (Aug 2025), but this is anecdotal — [ManyChat community](https://community.manychat.com/general-q-a-43/auto-dm-via-instagram-comments-not-working-after-applying-meta-monetization-7112).
- Sends per reach is a top signal for non-follower reach, so "envie para..." CTAs target the signal Instagram cites — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/).

### Inferences
- Caption template (pt-BR, professional tone per the brand's copy rules):
  - Line 1 (hook, under about 125 characters, visible before "mais"): restate the cover benefit with the main keyword ("Como gravar o melhor lance da sua partida de tênis sem parar o jogo").
  - Line break, then 2-4 short lines of value or context with secondary keywords in natural language.
  - CTA line, choosing one: "Salve para usar no próximo jogo" / "Envie para seu parceiro de dupla" / "Comente CLIPE e enviamos o link".
  - Hashtag line: 3-5 tags (see section 4).
- Use short paragraphs with line breaks, and 0-3 functional emojis as bullets. I found no 2025-2026 data on emoji effects, so this is a style choice.
- Use one CTA per post. Rotate save, send and comment prompts according to the post type (saves for tutorials, sends for relatable or funny posts, comments for questions).
- Comment-to-DM is useful at launch for "comente APP e receba o link da App Store". Use an official-API tool, keep the keyword unique, and also reply publicly in the comments.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 large-sample caption-length study for brand accounts. The Buffer caption study was not found.
- No reliable engagement data on comment-to-DM. Effectiveness claims come only from vendors.
- No data on emoji use or line-break formatting in 2025-2026.

## 4. Hashtags and Instagram SEO: limits, keywords, alt text, name field, Google indexing

### Takeaway
Hashtags are now capped at 5 per post or Reel (Dec 2025). Mosseri says they do not meaningfully boost reach and work as categorization. Discovery now runs on keywords: handle, name field, bio, captions, on-screen text and alt text. Since July 10, 2025, public posts from professional accounts (18+) can be indexed by Google and Bing, so captions double as web SEO.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram capped hashtags at **5 per post/Reel**, announced 2025-12-18 and rolling out globally (previously 30) — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/no-more-hashtag-stuffing-instagram-limits-hashtag-usage-to-just-5-per-reel-and-post-507444-2025-12-19); [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/instagram-caps-hashtag-usage-to-5-per-post-here-s-why/story). Unclear: whether hashtags in comments count toward the 5. Earlier tests limited it to 3 — [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/blog/instagram-is-limiting-hashtags.md).
- **Stale info alert:** Some 2026 pages still cite 30 hashtags per carousel caption — [Adaptly](https://adaptlypost.com/pt/blog/instagram-carousel-limit). This is outdated.
- Mosseri: hashtags do not boost reach meaningfully, there is no "night-and-day difference", and they work as a categorization tool — [Phable](https://www.phable.io/phable-labs/instagram-hashtags-keywords-discovery); [Corkboard Concepts](https://corkboardconcepts.com/marketing-resources/blog/are-hashtags-still-relevant-in-2026/). These are secondary sources, and I did not find the original Q&A.
- Brazilian practitioner advice: Instagram recommends 3-5 tags, and the author reviews hashtag sets every 30-45 days — [Jamile Fernandes](https://jamilefernandes.com.br/blog/hashtags-para-instagram-como-usar-de-forma-estrategica-em-2026). Her claim that 5-10 works in practice is now moot given the cap.
- Placement: there is no solid evidence that hashtags perform differently in the caption vs the first comment — [SocialKit](https://socialk.it/pt/blog/instagram-carousel-strategy).
- Keyword placement for Instagram search: handle, profile name (name field), bio and captions, attributed to Mosseri via secondary sources. The name field is searchable and can hold keywords separately from the @handle (about 30 characters, unverified). Alt text helps accessibility and search. Auto alt text is generic, so write your own under Advanced settings, and avoid stuffing — [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-seo/); [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-seo); [SEO.com](https://www.seo.com/blog/instagram-seo/).
- **Google/Bing indexing:** From 2025-07-10, public photos, videos, Reels and carousels from professional (business/creator) accounts of users 18+ can appear in search engines, for posts from 2020 on. It is on by default, with an opt-out setting ("Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results"). Stories and Highlights are not indexed — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/instagram-content-becomes-searchable-on-google-starting-july-10/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/); [Search Engine World](https://www.searchengineworld.com/its-live-instagram-posts-are-ranking-and-showing-in-google-and-bing-seo-meets-social). I did not find Meta's primary announcement, and some sources note public posts were partly indexed before.

### Inferences
- Use **3-5 hashtags**: 1 brand tag (#reprize), plus 2-4 specific topic tags (sport and use case in pt-BR, for example #beachtennis #padel #tenisamador). Skip generic mega-tags.
- Keyword map: choose 1 primary keyword per post (for example "gravar jogo de tênis", "melhores lances beach tennis"). Put it in the first caption line, in the alt text, and as visible text on the cover. Google indexing makes the first caption line act like a title tag.
- Alt text template: "[O que aparece] + [contexto] + [palavra-chave]". For example: "Jogador de beach tennis com Apple Watch revendo o clipe do ponto no iPhone, app Reprize".
- Make sure the account is professional (creator or business) and public, and leave search-engine indexing on.

### Gaps
- Whether comment hashtags count toward the 5 cap is unclear.
- Relative weighting of name field vs bio vs caption is not public.
- No primary Meta source on the indexing rules or the hashtag cap was found in this pass, so verify both in the Help Center.

## 5. Design patterns that perform, and what transfers from developer/startup brands

### Takeaway
Performing patterns are bold, legible text overlays on the cover, one idea per slide, real photos and real product screens over generic graphics, consistent templates, and save-worthy or send-worthy formats (how-tos, checklists, relatable humor). Rigorous data on faces or photos vs graphics is not available. Brand examples (Duolingo and others) were thinly sourced in this pass.

### Cited Findings
- Same colors, fonts and layout across slides. Split complex topics into one point per slide — [Adaptly PT](https://adaptlypost.com/pt/blog/text-overlays-on-instagram-carousels).
- Make every slide worth saving or sending on its own, and front-load the strongest slides (driven by the re-serve) — [Social Boost Digital](https://socialboostdigital.com/blog/instagram-carousel-algorithm-double-exposure).
- Behind-the-scenes and personal stories humanize brands — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/how-to-create-engaging-instagram-carousels/522723/).
- Duolingo: an always-on, mascot-led (Duo) content and community strategy with rapid trend reaction (24-48h) and a self-aware character — [HSE Design analysis](https://pro.hsedesign.ru/project/duolingo-communication-strategy-analysis-f8677bc0d8aa430b91e0a509cf4906b2) (low-authority). The claim that it fully rejected educational content conflicts with its positioning, so treat it with caution.
- Instagram reads on-image text for search and discovery — [Phable](https://www.phable.io/phable-labs/instagram-hashtags-keywords-discovery) (secondary).
- Vendor claims such as "carousels get 3x more engagement" are unreliable, given that benchmark gaps between formats are 0.03-0.18 points in Socialinsider's data — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-carousel-posts-study/).

### Inferences
- What transfers to a small sports-tech app:
  - From Strava/NRC-type brands (my characterization, not sourced in this pass): real athletes and real sessions. Use real field-test footage and clips, with UI shown in context (a watch on the wrist, a phone at the court).
  - From Duolingo: a consistent voice or character and quick reaction to moments. In a sports niche that means reacting to big tournament moments with "this lance, in Reprize".
  - From Notion/Linear-type brands (my characterization, not sourced): restrained, consistent templates and product-led tutorials ("3 jeitos de usar...").
- Build 3-4 reusable Figma/Canva templates at 1080x1440: (a) a tutorial carousel, (b) a "lance da semana" real photo or clip with a short overlay, (c) a myth/fact or error/fix comparison, (d) a launch or update announcement. Fix the type scale (cover headline at about 90-120px, body at about 40-48px on a 1080 canvas) and use the Placar palette (these sizes are practitioner convention, not sourced).
- Text density: cover at 5-9 words. Inner slides with max about 25-30 words, or 1 headline plus 1-3 lines.

### Gaps
- No reliable 2025-2026 data on faces vs no faces, photo vs graphic, or text-overlay density effects.
- I found no sources on the Instagram strategies of Notion, Linear, Strava or Nike Run Club. Brand lessons above beyond Duolingo are my own inference and need a dedicated search.

## 6. Bio optimization: name field, bio formula, link in bio, highlights

### Takeaway
The name field and bio are searchable keyword slots. Keep the bio clear and keyword-rich, use one external bio link (or a link-in-bio page), and use Highlights for evergreen "start here" content (Highlights are not indexed by Google).

### Cited Findings
- The name field (bold name, separate from the @handle) is searchable and should contain a keyword. It can be changed without changing the handle (about 30 characters, unverified) — [Later](https://later.com/blog/instagram-seo/); [Kapwing](https://kapwing.com/resources/instagram-seo-the-ultimate-guide-to-gaining-visibility).
- The bio should be clear, keyword-rich and easy to read at a glance. The bio link is the profile's main external link — [Torro](https://torro.io/blog/instagram-profile-tips); [SocialKit](https://socialk.it/en/blog/instagram-pinned-posts-strategy).
- Stories and Highlights are not indexed by search engines — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/).

### Inferences
- Name field template: "Reprize | clipes do seu jogo" or "Reprize • Replay de tênis e padel" (brand plus keyword, within the limit).
- Bio formula, 3 lines plus a link: (1) what it is plus who it is for ("Grave os melhores lances da sua partida pelo Apple Watch"), (2) proof or differentiator, (3) CTA ("Baixe na App Store ↓"), then the link (App Store or a simple link page with UTM parameters).
- Highlights: "Comece aqui", "Como usar", "Clipes", "Novidades", with consistent brand-color covers.

### Gaps
- No official source on the name-field character limit or search weighting.
- No 2025-2026 data comparing link-in-bio tools vs a direct App Store link.
- The current limit on bio links (Instagram allowed up to 5 links since 2023, from training knowledge, not verified in this pass).
